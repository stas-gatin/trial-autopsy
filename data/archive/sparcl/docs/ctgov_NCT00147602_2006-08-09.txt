ClinicalTrials.gov record NCT00147602 (SPARCL)
Title: Lipitor In The Prevention Of Stroke, For Patients Who Have Had A Previous Stroke
Official title: A Double-Blind, Randomized, Placebo- Controlled Study Of Atorvastatin As Prevention Of Cerebrovascular Events In Patients With A Previous Transient Ischemic Attack (TIA) Or Stroke
Sponsor: Pfizer
Phase: PHASE4
Conditions: Cardiovascular Disease
Interventions: atorvastatin (DRUG)
Start date: 1998-11
Enrollment: 4732 (None)
Primary outcome(s): Time to occurrence of fatal or non-fatal stroke
Eligibility criteria:
Inclusion Criteria:

* Previous stroke or TIA

Exclusion Criteria:

* coronary heart disease
Brief summary: To determine whether Lipitor reduces stroke, compared to placebo in patients who have had a previous stroke or transient ischemic attack.
