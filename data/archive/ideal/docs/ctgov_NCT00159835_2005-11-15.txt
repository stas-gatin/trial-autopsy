ClinicalTrials.gov record NCT00159835 (IDEAL)
Title: Atorvastatin Versus Simvastatin In The Prevention Of Coronary Heart Disease (CHD) In Patients With Known CHD
Official title: Atorvastatin Compared With Simvastatin In The Prevention of CHD Morbidity And Mortality In Patients With CHD
Sponsor: Pfizer
Phase: PHASE4
Conditions: Cardiovascular Diseases
Interventions: atorvastatin (DRUG); simvastatin (DRUG)
Start date: 1999-02
Enrollment: 8600 (None)
Primary outcome(s): Time to of a major coronary event: 1) non-fatal acute myocardial infarction 2) coronary death 3) resuscitated cardiac arrest
Eligibility criteria:
Inclusion Criteria:

* Previous myocardial infarction

Exclusion Criteria:

* Women who are breast feeding or are pregnant
Brief summary: To investigate whether a long-term strategy to lower LDL cholesterol with atorvastatin as much as possible will improve prognosis in CHD patients compared with a strategy reflecting current best clinical practice with simvastatin.
